## Direction contract

THESIS: Every project is a street poster announcing its release, pasted on one sidewalk-shed wall, newest on top; below the wall the same work is set straight on printed paper. Refuses the dark hero, card grid and logo wall.

OWN-WORLD: Hunter-green plywood with seams and a POST NO BILLS stencil; posters in black, paper white and fluorescent pink, yellow and orange; paste gloss, wrinkles, torn edges; condensed wood-type display over a plain grotesque. Paper sheets are neutral newsprint with black rules and printed status chips. Raised by the cracktro challenger: real screenshots print unretouched, only the paper wears.

STORY: The visitor sees every project at once, reads each status, peels a poster to reach its print entry, reads the program, the résumé sheet, then tears a tab or sends the flyer.
